# Accounts and gallery

Part of the [garden design app](../garden-design-app/intent-brief.md). This document owns accounts, sharing, and the public gallery. See the [intent index](../README.md) for the other parts.

## My read

People must not lose their work, and they want to show it to family and friends. Accounts make sure a project is always there when its owner comes back. The account system stays as simple as possible. Sharing lets a family member see a garden. The public gallery lets anyone browse gardens that owners choose to show, as a zoomed-out wall of small gardens that you zoom into.

## What matters most

- **Never lose work.** A project is saved to the owner's account.
- **Simple.** Email and password, and the simplest database.
- **Private until the owner chooses.** Nothing is public unless the owner marks it public, and the public view never shows where someone lives.

## The experience you appear to want

You sign up with an email and a password. Your projects are in your account, and they are there when you return.

You share a project with another person's account. They see every view and the time slider, but they cannot edit.

You mark a project public. It joins the gallery: a wall of garden thumbnails, zoomed out. Anyone can pan the wall and zoom in to open one garden.

## Boundaries

### Must be true

- Accounts use email and password only.
- A shared project is view only.
- A project is private until its owner marks it public.
- The public view hides the address and the aerial photo.

### Must be avoided

- Magic links, private links, or other login methods.
- Payments in the first version.
- Any public view that shows where a person lives.

## What seems settled

- The gallery is a zoomable wall of thumbnails, not only a list.

## Possibilities, not decisions

- Community use later, such as a city like Fishers planning a quiet public space.
- A public portfolio for landscape architects, and a garden explainer such as Versailles over time.
- A professional tier.
