# Plant library

Part of the [garden design app](../garden-design-app/intent-brief.md). This document owns the plants: their facts, how they grow, and the gallery people pick from. See the [intent index](../README.md) for the other parts.

## My read

Every design, warning, time-slider frame, and planting plan depends on trusted facts about each plant. The first version knows about 100 popular Indiana plants very well, rather than thousands of plants poorly. A beginner who does not know where to start sees popular, easy, local plants first. An experienced gardener finds the plant they love and builds around it.

The gallery is visual. People browse plants as pictures, filter them, and drag them onto the plan.

## What matters most

- **Facts are real.** Each fact has a source. The AI never invents a plant fact.
- **Time is in the data.** Size over the years, bloom months, and the look in each season drive the time slider.
- **Effort is clear.** Annual or perennial, lifespan, and yearly work, such as lifting dahlias in the fall, are visible before you plant.

## The experience you appear to want

You open the plant gallery. You filter by color, bloom time, sun, and effort. Each plant shows its watercolor art and its key facts: how big it gets, how long it takes, when it blooms, and how much work it needs. You drag one onto the plan and paint an area with it.

Plants that are safe near kids and pets, and plants that bring birds, hummingbirds, or butterflies, are easy to find. Low-effort choices include perennials, shrubs, and artificial plants that never die.

## Boundaries

### Must be true

- The library starts with about 100 popular Indiana plants.
- Each plant has: size and spread at maturity, years to reach it, sun, soil, water, bloom months and colors, look by season, annual or perennial, lifespan, hardiness zone, care by month, planting window, lift and store, thorns, toxicity to kids and pets, wildlife value, and effort.
- Each fact has a source.
- Artificial plants are in the library as a low-effort option.

### Must be avoided

- Plant facts that the AI makes up.
- A huge catalog that is shallow and hard to trust.

## What seems settled

- Indiana only. Popular, easy, local plants come first for beginners.
- A simple growth curve and a seasonal look drive the time slider (spike S11).

## Possibilities, not decisions

- Growth simulation later: seed to flower, and plants that fill in or crowd out others.
- Plants for other regions later.
